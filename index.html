THE HPL WORLD / THE INTELLIGENCE OF NATURE

Nature shows us
how to grow together.

Biomimicry inspires the identity, spaces and materials. The T4HPL methodology guides learning experiences. Together, they give coherence to how the school looks, feels and works.

05 / A MODEL FOR THE LONG TERM

01 / THE INVESTMENT

A partner-backed beginning.

02 / CAPITAL RECOVERY

Priority for invested capital.

The proposal directs net profit towards recovery of the initial investment, under the agreed contractual terms.

03 / SHARED GROWTH

Building value together.

After capital recovery, the reference proposal is a 50/50 profit-sharing model, with shared strategic decisions.

Indicative structure for discussion. Financial and legal arrangements, the definition of profit and distributions are subject to feasibility analysis and validation of the operating structure.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Let’s start
with clarity.

Who is this partnership for?

Property developers and investors who want to integrate an educational campus into a community and participate in its long-term development.

Who runs the school?

HPL Schools provides operational management and educational coordination. The partner participates in shared governance, strategy and performance reviews within the agreed contractual framework.

What is an HPL-managed franchise?

It is the proposed expansion model in which the campus uses the HPL brand and concept, with operations coordinated by the HPL team. Territory, duration, responsibilities, any fees and standards are defined in each partnership agreement.

Is there a minimum investment or a guaranteed return?

Investment is calculated for the location, capacity and phases of the campus. Projections are discussed through a feasibility analysis; this page does not offer guaranteed returns or a fixed capital recovery period.

How are school stages and opening dates determined?

The proposed pathway covers kindergarten, primary, middle and high school through a Learning Hub. Opening and expansion are planned around local demand, resources, space and the approvals required for each stage.
